Week 2 Power Rankings

North Andover enters the top 10, Arlington joins the rankings, and Division 1B continues to heat up.

By Ethan Barossi · September 21, 2026
North Andover boys after winning the Amherst Invitational.

After only two full weeks, we're starting to get a real sense of where most of the teams and individuals stand heading into the end of September. This week in particular brought a lot of movement, especially inside Division 1B, where there are now four teams that look capable of making a run at a trophy.

With several big meets coming up during Week 3, we're expecting these rankings to look vastly different by next week. However, that doesn't mean we can't stop and take a look at the teams and individuals who impressed us over the past week.

Division 1B Is Getting Heated

North Andover enters the top 10 after a great performance and win at the Amherst Invitational this past weekend. We already mentioned the amount of depth they showed at the Clipper Relays, but this performance showed us that they have plenty of individual power too.

They went 5–8 at the invitational, placing four runners inside the top 10. Tucker LaRosa (16:43) led the squad individually, while Liam Rand (16:46), Owen Desmand (16:48), and Cameron Riccio (16:52) followed right behind him. Keep in mind, that's only nine seconds separating their top four runners.

The craziest part about this pack is that their coach stated they were also missing two of their top runners. We're assuming he's talking about William Thomas (9:49) and Tristan Rand (10:07). Once this North Andover pack gets to full strength, they should have their eyes on challenging Natick and Concord-Carlisle for the D1B title.

Don't think North Andover is the only D1B team that surprised us this week, though, as Arlington enters the rankings at #13.

We talked about this team in our preseason honorable mentions, but we didn't think they would open nearly as well as they did. We mentioned that Owen Merrill (16:07) would be leading this squad as their #1, but he now has two more runners right behind him.

We didn't see anything like this coming, but two more seniors, Eli Hanover (16:10) and Nathan Wilson (16:23), are following right behind Merrill, giving Arlington a similar trio to what they had last year. Zachery Weinberg is also a solid #4 runner for the squad, opening in 16:40, while Evan Jacobson anchors the scoring group after opening in 17:16.

While North Andover has its eyes on Natick and Concord-Carlisle for the D1B title, Arlington is waiting right behind them.

Lexington opened its season this weekend at the Manchester Invitational. With terrain similar to Northfield Mountain, this course is very unpredictable, so we're not going to move Lexington around in the rankings based on this performance. However, there was still plenty of good to take away from the meet.

Trevor Stevens placed fourth overall in 16:13, while Patrick Nooan ran 16:43 to place 14th. To put those times into perspective, Brookline went to this meet during the 2024 season, where Altamo Aschkenasy ran 16:22 and Lysander Duffield ran 16:27.

Although we originally thought Stevens would be the squad's #2 runner, we're starting to rethink that. If the All-State meet were taking place within the next week, we feel confident that Stevens would be right in the mix with some of the state's top runners, including Ryan Costello, Anthony Malakidis, and Liam Hartmann.

Honorable Mentions

Three new teams opened strong and caught our attention this week. Although they don't crack the top 20, all three are sitting right on the cusp.

Wellesley opened on the fast terrain of Cold Spring Park and looked strong against one of the top teams in the state. Daniel Goldberg (16:13) is a solid front-runner for the squad, followed by two more runners below the 17:00 barrier. Jonas Wigneswarran anchors the squad, coming in at 17:17. Wellesley faces #20 Walpole in two weeks, and we're excited to see how the two teams match up.

Xaverian had a great opener where they nearly beat #7 St. John's Prep on its home course. Marco Petrino (16:54) and Alex Armstong (16:56) went 1–2 to open the race. Emile Brodeur (17:20) and Scott Feeney (17:25) then had strong finishes to help the squad against St. John's Prep.

Although Luke Herman (17:45) anchored the squad a little farther back, Xaverian's #1 runner, Jack Weintraub, didn't race. If he's fully healthy, Xaverian could be looking very strong entering October.

The third team is Marshfield, which has looked very strong in both meets it has competed in. Against Duxbury, its entire top five came in together at 17:28. We don't know whether this was all-out or a tempo effort, but the pack mentality they showed during that race is definitely one of their strengths.

We also know that their top runner, Connor Rightmire, can go a lot faster, having run 16:45 to place sixth at the MarshVegas Classic.
