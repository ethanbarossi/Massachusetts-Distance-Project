CROSS COUNTRY · WEEK IN REVIEW

Week in Review: October 12–17

A loaded Catholic Memorial Invitational, Arlington's upset over Reading, and pivotal conference battles across Massachusetts.

By Ethan Barossi · October 18, 2026

Catholic Memorial Invitational

Spotlight: Cambridge Rindge & Latin

With many big invitationals being cancelled in recent weeks, the CMI had a very large amount of talent compared to previous years.

Cambridge Rindge & Latin, the #1 ranked team in the state, finally opened their season this weekend and they did not disappoint. 3 runners under 15:50, 5 under 16:10, and on top of that they were missing one of their top returners.

Leon Ibanez-Fraile led the squad taking the individual win in a time of 15:37. Keep in mind that with the inclusion of Bearcage hill, Franklin park is definitely not an easy course. Taking a big win over Newton North’s Ryan Costello (15:40), Ibanez-Fraile entered contention to win the individual title at MOC in November.

Thomas DeWitt placed 5th overall, being the 3rd best runner from Massachusetts. He ran a time of 15:43 which was only 3 seconds behind Costello, and 6 seconds behind Ibanez-Fraile. Watch out for this duo come November as they have just as much top-end talent as Brookline.

Liam Eldridge (15:49) placed 10th overall being the 5th best runner from MA. Gael Berzin (16:07) and Henry Parker (16:09) rounded out this squad to both place inside the top 25. Earlier in the season we talked about how the key to Cambridge’s success would either be getting Parker fully healthy after his injury or getting Berzin down to the 16:20 range. Both these runners accomplished these goals and put their team in a great position.

Though this team is even better when you take into account their missing runner: Kieran Eichler. He comes into the season with a 16:03 PR and when he is fully healthy he should be a big player for the squad.

Spotlight: Concord-Carlisle

Concord-Carlisle had a much better performance than their races at the Bay State Invitational. Their entire team ran faster on a harder course with harder conditions. Braeden Quayle (15:49) led this squad followed by Toby Martel (16:04). Their big pack then came in with Sawyer Bout (16:19), Will Okie (16:20), Ryan Perry (16:22), Jack Thomas (16:22.4), and Elijah Liang (16:27).

This performance moves CC up in the rankings and makes the future battle for D1B even more exciting.

Spotlight: Natick, Newton North, Boston Latin Academy

The next MA team was actually Natick taking an upset victory over Newton North with a 6th and 7th place finish.

Newton North didn’t look that great with their pack having a very large spread. Lucas Allen was their #2 coming in at 16:30 while Jamie Evans was #7 coming in at 17:01. Though it is important to mention that Newton North was also out their #2 runner: Alex Daly.

Natick took advantage of their situation to get some revenge from earlier in the season. Declan Hava had a great race to place 13th overall in 15:50. Callum Doyle didn’t have his best day coming in at 16:13 for 27th. Benjamin Gidelson (16:27), Alex Forte (16:47), and **Dylan Cooke **(16:49), rounded out this squad.

Boston Latin Academy then improved their position from 2 weeks ago. Braden Spiess placed 9th in 15:48, the 4th best individual from MA. Adam Kramer ran 15:56 for 15th, and Khalid Jama ran 16:43. Though the main performance was Nate Iliff running 16:32. He improved his time by almost 30 seconds to make this team's top 4 runners even scarier.

#12 Arlington upsets #10 Reading 27–28

For the past few weeks we've been talking about how Reading Memorial could be one of the state’s top teams if they got their act together. However their streak of lackluster performances unfortunately cost them this meet.

Arlington narrowly escaped Reading on their opponent's home turf. Owen Merrill (16:01) and Zachery Weinberg (16:08) went 2-3 in the race to lead their team. Eli Hanover (16:18) then came 5th overall, barely outpaced by Reading’s Declan Grant (16:17). Evan Jacobson finished 7th in 16:34, while Nathan Wilson rounded out the squad in 17:04 for 10th.

Though this wasn’t Reading’s best, it's still better than what we’ve seen from the group over the past month. Finn Johnson-Houlihan is finally back after taking 1st place in a PR of 15:54. After his big spring track season we still think Johnson-Houlihan can run a lot faster. Declan Grant then followed with a big 26 second PR to place 4th overall and become the teams #2 runner. Colin Carroll then had another big PR of 19 seconds to finish 6th in 16:25. Colin Herlihy (16:38) and Curran Healy (16:41) rounded out the squad.

Andrew Princic had his first race back after injury to run 18:01. Watch out for Princic come post-season as he should be climbing the teams depth chart. For the second meet in a row Max King ended up not finishing the race. Once King and Princic get back to full strength this squad should look like the team we know they can be.

Congratulations to Arlington for winning the Liberty division of the M12 league.

Reading also celebrated their senior night. Congratulations to Johnson-Houlihan, King, Princic, Grant, Herlihy, Jacob Ryle, Quinn Kane, Luke Bishop, Gavin Ganann, and Griffin Fournier.

Oliver Ames is Back

A couple weeks ago we decided to take Oliver Ames off the rankings due to them having not given us a good performance. We doubted this team's ability to run a fast 5K or just put all their pieces together.

We were very wrong.

Devon McStowe (15:46) and Corey Yifru **(15:53) went 1-2 in this race. That's a crazy 1-2 punch for any team. **Peter Feodoroff (16:37) and Jake Hauvey **(16:47) followed behind to place 4th and 5th. **Nicholas Colvin surprised us with a big performance to place 7th in 16:59.

Another reason we moved OA out of the top 20 was due to them being out one of their top returners in Colin Donahue. Although we thought they wouldn’t be able to cover their depth, Colvin’s performance proves that we were again very mistaken.

In what looked like it could’ve been a close meet with Mansfield, Oliver Ames won with a score of 19-36. There were also a few key performances from Mansfield in Nikita Cherapukha (16:00) and freshman Barrett Reynolds (16:58).

Mansfield also celebrated their senior night on Tuesday. Congratulations to Walter Stock and Jacob Hyre.

#17 Longmeadow Conquers Western Mass

Course note: Longmeadow’s home course appears closer to three miles than a full 5K.

On Friday, #17 Longmeadow took a big win over #19 Amherst-Pelham 19 - 38.

Although this doesn’t look like Amherst-Pelham’s best day, Longmeadow had quite the performance. Jacob Abebe is finally back to full strength after taking first place in 15:45, beating some fast individuals like CJ Deliso (16:09) and** Calvin Miller** (16:47).

Deliso went on to place 2nd in the race. He ran 15:35 on this course earlier this season so it’s hard to tell if this was a workout or just an off day. Finn O’Connor, the state runner-up in the pentathlon, then placed 3rd in 16:37.

Miller then came in after that with teammate Otis Fairey (16:42). These two might have just been doing a workout but we are not quite sure. Miller just edged out Longmeadow’s Henry Cournoyer **(16:47), and teammate Aidan Kretschmar** (16:53) followed right behind to round out the Longmeadow squad.

It is also important to note that Amherst-Pelham’s Peter Nadeau did not enter the race.

Needham Edges Out Walpole (27-30)

Walpole, Needham and Milton had a very close tri-meet on Wednesday to decide the fate of the Bay State Herget.

Milton’s Bobby Beato **led from gun to the line to take first place in 13:29 on the 2.57 mile course. Needham’s **Connor Bobbin (13:37) then broke away from the pack to place 2nd overall. Walpole’s Nate O’Neil (13:43) then battled Needham’s **Austin Conti **(13:48) to place 3rd and 4th.

Walpole’s Joseph Curran (13:55) then came in with teammate Ethan Hamilton (13:58). Needham’s** Cash Lindsley** (13:59) and Milton’s Nate Turner (14:00) followed closely behind.

Needham ended up taking a big win over Walpole with some strong top-end talent and getting all 7 runners in before Walpole’s #5 runner. Tyler Mann, Duncan Alexander, Kaden Hu, and Gabriele Mineo rounded out the Needham squad. Walpole also took the win over Milton 26 - 30.

Congratulations Needham for winning the Herget division of the Bay State Conference.

Congratulations to Walpole for their senior night celebration. Shoutout to** O’Neil, Curran, Asher Kingman, Finn Hession, Sean Larkin, and **Owen Ahlfont.

North Andover Conquers Billerica and Dracut

A pair of brothers from Dracut won the race overall in a 1-2 finish. Tyler Mogavero (16:35) and Ryan Mogavero (16:44) lead the Dracut squad from the front.

From there, North Andover didn’t just take control, they took dominance over the field. North Andover took the next 6 spots in the race, defeating MVC ranked #2 Billerica with only 15 points. Will Thomas (16:45) lead this squad followed by** Liam Rand** (16:47) and Tristand Rand (16:49). Owen Desmand (16:50), Tucker LaRosa (17:01), and **Cam Riccio **(17:02) rounded out this squad. That’s only a 17 second spread 1 - 6.

Although Billerica is one of the top teams in the state, it doesn’t look like they made an attempt against North Andover. This is fair considering how deep North Andover really goes. Sahil Gandhi (17:04) placed 10th overall in what looks to be a tempo. Mason Niles placed 12th in 17:14, falling off Ghandi by quite a bit.

Lexington destroys Winchester 15–50

Lexington swept the Winchester team on their own senior night.

Alden Hall took the win on the course in 16:26.** Trevor Stevens** (16:31) and** Patrick Noonan (16:31) followed closely behind in what looks to be a tempo effort. **Kiran Green (17:05), Benjamin Sbihli (17:10), Matteo Sanchez de Rojas (17:11), Ethan Wang **(17:12), and **Andrew Dulgarian (17:16) rounded out the squad.

Congratulations to Hall, Stevens, Noonan, Sbihli, Owen Ross, Gabriel Ostrower, Finn O’Donnell, Patrick Puscasu, Gabriel Alves, Benjamin Davis, Bennett Webster, Alessandro Vicentini, and Ivan Wu.

Pembroke Defeats Scituate 19–44

Pembroke’s Logan Holloway (16:06) took the win on the 2.75 mile course. Don’t sleep on Holloway as this looked more like a tempo effort than anything else. Holloway ran the exact same time over 5 kilometers at the Twilight Invitational last weekend.

Scituate’s Steven Capone followed closely behind to run 16:14. After that Pembroke took the next 7 spots. Sam Ruggiero (16:22), Marco Gulinello (16:46), Benjamin Meehan (16:46), Daniel Finn (17:01), Michael Henry (17:09), and **Logan Pope **(17:20) rounded out the squad.

With this victory Pembroke wins the Patriot League Fisher division and celebrates its senior night. Congratulations to Nate Palmieri, Royal Nelson, Gavin Campbell, and Daniel Gallagher.

Marshfield takes down Hingham 16–45

Marshfield destroys the competition to take the Keenan title inside the Patriot League.

Conor Rightmire **(16:27) led the squad, followed by **Archie Meberg (16:36), Cal Kennedy **(16:45), and **Peter Adams (17:05). Hingham’s Will Watts took 5th place in 17:15.

With this win Marshfield should have their eyes on the overall Patriot League title.

This team also celebrated their senior night. Congratulations to Caleb O’Callahan, Jake Pitts, Kyle Paris, Aidan Campbell, CJ Kennedy, Michael Zides, and Robbie Jorden.

Natick handles Weymouth 20–41

Patrick Geagan (16:34) took the individual win here followed by 5 more Natick runners. Callum Doyle (16:56), Declan Hava (16:56), Benjamin Gidelson (17:07), Jonathan Pereira (17:22), and Kai Vickers-Richard (17:32) completed this squad. Weymouth’s** Andrew Himmer** followed right behind in 17:33.

Natick also celebrated their senior night. Congratulations to Doyle, Hava, Gidleson, Pereira, John Power, Aidan Ristey, Max Retik, Nicholas Mineo, Evan Garber, Max Nahas, Raphael Camerini, and Gevorg Gentile.

Brookline Tempos to Victory Against Braintree and Framingham

Liam Hartmann (17:24) took the overall win with Matt Ritter **(17:25) and **Ibrahim Abdel-Dayem (17:26) right behind him. Brookline’s Axel Bonnafoux (17:36) and Braintree’s Kyle Koca (17:41) followed closely behind.

Elias Thomasson (17:43), Nam Danh (17:45), and Jack Hochleunter (17:48), rounded out Brookline’s top 7.

Coming off a win at both the Frank Kelley and Twilight Invitationals, Brookline’s stud freshman Kieran Morrisey-Dhenke, had his varsity debut where he ran 17:51 for 10th place overall.

The Warriors come back on Wednesday for one of the most highly anticipated duel meets of the season. #2 Brookline will face off against #5 Newton North on their own home turf of Larz Anderson Park.

Could the warriors finally take the #1 spot in the rankings, or could Newton North pull off the upset of the year?
