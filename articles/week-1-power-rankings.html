Week 1 Power Rankings

BC High and Newton North climb, North Andover crashes the party, and MDP expands its rankings to a Top 20.
By Ethan Barossi · September 14, 2026

As the preseason ends and meets start happening all over the state, we’re starting to get a real sense of where teams truly stand. This article begins our weekly power rankings featuring the best teams across Massachusetts.

Although during the preseason we only ranked the top 15, we looked at the results from this past week and decided that 15 spots simply weren’t enough. There’s too much talent in Massachusetts.

BC High Makes a Statement at the Clipper Relays

This Saturday, the BC High team started its season by placing third at the Clipper Relays, only losing to Billerica and North Andover. Based on this performance, the Eagles jumped from #6 to #4.

One of the biggest reasons for this was their win over #2 Lexington and #7 St. John’s Prep while missing one of their top runners. Owen Geagan, who we’ve stated has the potential to be running up with Colin Kurtz, was not a part of the third-place relay this weekend. Instead, Kurtz was joined by Joseph Hayes and Theo Weiss.

BC High is starting its season on a high note and hopes to keep that momentum rolling.

Newton North Doesn’t Disappoint

During the preseason, we stated that Newton North might be one of the biggest sleeper teams in the state. However, this team isn’t sleeping anymore.

Newton North jumps to #6 after a big win over #9 Natick. Up front, Ryan Costello and Alex Daly immediately started their seasons with big PRs. Even though they were missing their #2 returner, this didn’t stop the rest of the team from stepping up. Four runners who were not in their projected top seven helped take the win over Natick and Walpole: Colin Van Vliet, Reed Smith, Edward Howell, and Lucca Biello.

Newton North starts its season already having all seven runners under 17:00 and the current MA #1 5K. We stated that Newton North entered this year with potential, but after only one week, potential is becoming results.

North Andover Crashes the Party

Looking back at their times, we were definitely sleeping on this team during the preseason. However, their performance at the Clipper Relays this weekend completely blew our expectations out of the water.

They go from unranked to #11.

They showed serious top-end strength with a big second-place finish at the Clipper Relays, beating top-tier teams like BC High and Lexington. Though that isn’t the only reason they made the rankings. North Andover also finished sixth and eighth overall, with their B team beating out #7 St. John’s Prep.

Their performance at this meet tells us something special: this team is deep. We already know they are returning five runners who ran 10:07 or below for two miles, but there’s a high chance they have a lot more talent on this team.

Amherst-Pelham at #17

This team started off its season with a tempo effort last week, but they still have lots of potential inside Division 2 and Western Mass. Although they return four solid runners, their main problem is their #5. If this team can find another scorer, they could take a big leap in the rankings.

Boston Latin Academy Wants Its Title Back

Boston Latin Academy came away with the title this weekend at the MSTCA XC Relays. Nate Iliff, Brandon Spiess, and Adam Kramer combined for the title in 23:02.07. The craziest part about this is that they also have another runner who is sub-9:50 in the two-mile, Khalid Jama. They have some crazy star power up front, but just like Amherst, they need a solid #5. If they can fix that issue, we are confident they will be in the running to defend as state champions.

They enter the rankings at #18.

Billerica Has the Scariest Top End in the State

Yes, Billerica just won the Clipper Relays, and yes, multiple teams that placed behind them are higher on the list. Both things can be true for this team.

Billerica easily has one of the fastest trios in the state with Mason Niles, Sahil Ghandi, and Dylan Kane. Though after that, they have some issues. While the teams just mentioned only have a weak #5, Billerica also has a weak #4. Until we get a better idea of their back end, it’ll be tough to move them higher.

The Walpole Pack Has Returned

Walpole takes the final spot on the list after a huge opener where they defeated #9 Natick 25–30.

They had four runners ranging from 16:47–17:01. That’s only 14 seconds. The craziest part about this is that their #5, Owen Ahlfont, ran 17:23 to round out the squad on Wednesday. However, his PR from last year is actually 16:48, and he also holds the fastest two-mile time in Walpole. So this team actually has five runners capable of being within 14 seconds of each other.

If this pack continues moving forward and becomes even more tightly packed, they could be extremely difficult to score against.
