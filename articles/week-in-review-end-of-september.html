Week in Review: End of September

Upsets, breakout performances, and some of the deepest team showings of the season close out September across Massachusetts.

By Ethan Barossi  •  October 1, 2026
Natick’s Declan Hava (left) and Callum Doyle (right) crossing the line together to take the win over Needham.

#13 Xaverian upsets #7 BC High

Xaverian continued its streak this week with another upset win against a talented BC High team. With BC High out their top two runners, Xaverian took full advantage of their situation.

BC High still had some strong showings though. Joseph Hayes (16:33) and Theo Weiss (16:40) rebounded from their performances last week to place 2nd and 4th overall. Nolan Kurtz then went on to place 6th, getting right in the middle of the Xaverian pack.

Although we are pretty confident that Owen Geagan is currently dealing with injury, we are starting to suspect their #1 runner, Colin Kurtz, might be as well. Kurtz ran his last race two weeks ago and hasn’t been seen since. Unfortunately, without their top-end talent, BC High does move further down in the rankings.

On to Xaverian, we predicted last week that Jack Weintraub was capable of going under the 16:00 barrier in a 5K, and he proved himself this week even on the harsh terrain of Franklin Park. MDP’s Runner of the Week won the meet overall in 15:58, a PR of more than 30 seconds.

Once again, Xaverian showed off their depth from there. Alex Armstrong (16:34) finished third overall with Marco Petrino (16:48), Scott Feeney (16:54), and Emile Brodeur (17:01) all finishing within the top 8.

Xaverian has gone on a crazy streak these past few weeks. They’ve upset talented teams like BC High and Catholic Memorial while barely losing to the #3 ranked team in the state, St. John’s Prep. We’re excited to see if Xaverian can keep their momentum going at the end of the season, particularly at the Catholic Conference Championship and Division 1B.

Belmont upsets #8 Reading

We won’t claim that the Reading squad went into this race at full strength; however, we know that this squad is capable of a lot more.

Two of the Rockets’ top three runners didn’t cross the finish line, with Max King and Andrew Princic being absent or not finishing the race. It was also a bit of a surprise to see Declan Grant leading this squad, as he beat out teammate Finn Johnson-Houlihan, who is one of the state’s top returners over two miles.

Still, the boys from Belmont took full advantage of their situation, sealing the meet before the first Reading runner crossed the finish line. Noah Geiger (15:49) led the squad again this week to take home first. Ishaan Kanagaraj (15:52) and William Olmsted (15:59) followed closely behind to secure the top three positions and the team win.

Based on his performance from last week, it was surprising to see Olmsted up running with the top two; however, he is not to be understated. He comes into the season with a 10:03 PR over two miles and a 17th-place finish at the D1B meet from last year, barely missing the podium. Belmont now has a very powerful trio of runners up front going into October.

Both these teams have big matchups next week in some of the tightest meets in the Middlesex this year. Belmont will be heading over to face #11 Arlington while Reading is facing off against #6 Lexington.

Newton North is ready

We’ve been saying it all season, but this team just doesn’t stop. We knew Newton North was going to be a powerhouse team, but it’s only the end of September and we feel they’re ready to challenge the best the state has to offer.

Ryan Costello won the meet in a tempo effort of 16:17. Yes, a tempo. He then led three of his teammates to some big PRs. Alex Daly (16:18), Colin Van Vliet (16:19), and Mathew Gallo (16:19) all rounded out the top four of the race. Van Vliet had a big 25-second PR and Gallo PR’d by 19 seconds.

What happened next is hard to put into words. Newton North then put another NINE runners under the 17:00 barrier, with their #14 runner, Max Yee, running 17:01.

Jamie Evans, Lucas Allen, Edward Howell, Eli Billings, Lucca Biello, Ken Matsuoka, Will Keneally, Reed Smith, and Jonah Penn are all playing key roles inside what’s currently the deepest team in the state.

Before this race we thought Newton North was a bit further back. Now, we believe they are up there with some of the top teams in the state like Concord-Carlisle, St. John’s Prep, and Brookline. This team actually changed up their schedule, now going to the Twilight Invitational on October 11th. Can they enter the conversation?

Amherst-Pelham holds off Northampton

Two of the deepest squads out in Western Mass were pitted against each other, with Amherst taking a big win over Northampton by taking the top four spots. Calvin Miller (17:16), Peter Nedeau (17:27), Otis Fairey (17:29), and Nicholas Workman (17:38) all finished within 22 seconds of each other to secure the win.

Northampton rebounded by taking the next six spots. Although they aren’t at the same level as Amherst-Pelham, they are definitely one of the deeper teams in Western Mass. Henry Daggot (17:45) and Gus Frey (17:45) led the squad, with their entire top six finishing within 20 seconds of each other. That’s an impressive spread for any team.

Billerica wins their senior night.

Billerica hosted their senior night on Wednesday against a couple of teams, though the main battle was with Andover.

Andover’s Landon Zoss (13:29) caught us completely by surprise. He’s the #1 returner for the D1A race and the #6 returner for the Meet of Champions. This week he edged out Billerica’s Mason Niles (13:29) by less than a second on Niles’s own turf. Both individuals will match up again this weekend at Wrentham.

Sahil Ghandi, the state’s #2 returner over two miles, finished third overall in 13:42. Our assumption is that he went out with the top two runners but fell off a bit. Two more Billerica freshmen then finished inside the top six: Dylan Kane (14:11) and Shaunak Kulkarni (14:12). It was a bit of a surprise to see Billerica has another freshman running up with Kane; however, that just reaffirms their status as a top-20 team in the state.

#2 Brookline tempos a win over Wellesley and Walpole

Just like Newton North, Brookline is also ready to go. Walpole and Wellesley are two very impressive teams, so Brookline’s light effort in the win caught us by surprise. They placed five of their runners inside the top seven spots in times that don’t nearly look all out.

Elias Thomasson was the first Warrior to cross the line, finishing 2nd in 16:58. Two of the top runners in the state followed right behind him, with Liam Hartmann and Ibrahim Abdel-Dayem both running 16:59. Noah Karp (17:05) and Matt Ritter (17:06) rounded out the squad, placing 6th and 7th overall.

Although it was supposed to be a close meet between Walpole and Wellesley, the Walpole team did not have any response to the challenge. Wellesley’s Daniel Goldberg won the race overall in 16:42. Teammate Nate Flynn then barely lost to Brookline’s top three, running 17:00. Wellesley then went on to put four more runners in front of Walpole’s #3. Peter Mahoney (17:14), Conor Foley (17:23), Aidan Abazaj (17:27), and Jonas Wigneswaran rounded out this squad.

On the Walpole side, it was a bit surprising to see Joseph Curran leading the squad. He’s been their #3 runner for the entirety of September. Curran placed 8th overall, running 17:08.

#9 Natick defeats Needham.

Both the Needham and Natick squads sat half their runners, but Natick still took six of the top seven spots. If you didn’t believe in Natick’s depth, you should start rethinking your opinions.

Declan Hava (16:37) and Callum Doyle (16:37.3) crossed the line together in what looks to have been a tempo. Benjamin Gidelson was not far behind, sweeping the top three to run 16:53.

After that, three more Natick runners came in. Sasha Rennie placed 4th in 17:46.2. He was being chased down by Needham’s #1 runner, Austin Conti, who ran 17:46.9. This is Conti’s debut race of the season and he should climb up their depth chart as the season goes on. Connor Chviruk (17:52) and Jonah Greenberg (17:59) rounded out the Natick squad.

Marshfield defeats Silver Lake

3-0 Marshfield defeats Silver Lake to extend their undefeated record. Connor Rightmire took the win on the 2.97-mile course, running 16:05. Silver Lake’s Graham Laws followed closely behind, finishing 2nd in 16:22.

From there, Marshfield’s depth took over. They took the next three spots with Archie Meberg (16:41), Cal Kennedy (16:48), and Peter Adams (16:52) all coming in together. A few spots after that they had another tight pack, with Conor Keough, Emmett Auferio, and Caleb O’Callahan all coming in together between 18:00-18:09.

The Patriot League seems to be heading in a direction where the big matchup will be between Marshfield and Pembroke at their league meet. Marshfield will probably still take the win there. Although Pembroke has some heavy top end, they can’t currently compete with Marshfield’s depth.
