2026 Division 1: What to Expect

As we head into divisional championship week, we’ll be breaking down key teams and individuals that we believe can make some noise in the postseason. With some extremely tight divisions competing next weekend, you can expect plenty of great battles.

Division 1A

Team Seedings

  1. Brookline
  2. Cambridge Rindge & Latin
  3. St. John’s Prep
  4. BC High
  5. Lexington
  6. Newton North
  7. Newton South

Chase Pack: Needham, Andover, Lowell, Framingham

Although the main battle up front looks to be between Brookline and Cambridge, we think Brookline has the better shot at taking the title. Some may find this take a bit surprising because of how Cambridge looks on paper, but Brookline looks ready to challenge them.

One thing we’ve learned about Brookline over the past few years is that they don’t need to be the fastest team in the state, just the fastest team on race day. This team knows how to perform when it matters most.

Liam Hartmann and Ibrahim Abdel-Dayem are the top two seeds for the race. Although Cambridge’s runners look a bit farther back due to having only raced at Franklin Park, we’d still give the Brookline duo the edge. We also wouldn’t be surprised if we saw some of their other scorers break onto the podium. We’re specifically excited to see what Elias Thomasson and Matt Ritter can accomplish. Thomasson is coming off a great race at the Bay State Conference Championship, where he placed 6th overall in a PR of 15:58, while Ritter put down an impressive two-mile time of 9:40 earlier in the week.

Cambridge, however, is still a major threat that can’t be overlooked. They have four runners under 16:00, another at 16:09, and we still haven’t seen one of their top returners from last year. This won’t be easy for either team, as both look extremely strong entering the postseason.

We also wouldn’t sleep on either of the Catholic Conference teams. Although they’re a bit farther back from Brookline and Cambridge, you can expect a tight battle for 3rd and 4th. Both teams should be looking to beat one another after BC High’s upset victory last week.

With only one spot left after the top teams, the entire chase pack should be battling for a spot at the All-State Meet. Andover and Lowell probably have the biggest edge as of now, but expect a tight battle for 7th and 8th place.

Key Individuals

  • Liam Hartmann — Brookline
  • Ibrahim Abdel-Dayem — Brookline
  • Leon Ibanez-Fraile — Cambridge
  • Thomas Dewitt — Cambridge
  • Ryan Costello — Newton North
  • Colin Kurtz — BC High
  • Landon Zoss — Andover
  • Patrick Noonan — Lexington
  • Charlie Guthrie — Boston Latin

On the individual side, we’re pretty confident that the title will come down to six runners: Hartmann, Abdel-Dayem, Zoss, Kurtz, Ibanez-Fraile, and Noonan. All six have shown incredible strength throughout the season, with some major accomplishments.

Liam Hartmann is our favorite as of now. He enters the race as the No. 1 seed after running 15:23 at the Bay State Invitational, though we don’t think that performance was anywhere close to his full potential. We still believe Hartmann has a much faster race in him. Teammate Ibrahim Abdel-Dayem should also be right behind him after a great performance at their conference championship last weekend, where he beat out Hartmann for the title.

We also expect Landon Zoss and Patrick Noonan to be in the mix. They’re the No. 1 and No. 2 returners from this meet last year, where they placed 2nd and 3rd overall, upsetting Hartmann and Theodore Butty. Both have also put together impressive seasons, specifically at the MVC Championship and Middlesex 12 League Meet, where Zoss placed 2nd overall and Noonan became conference champion.

Colin Kurtz should also be a top contender. After a somewhat injury-heavy season, he showed his true potential at the Catholic Conference Championship, where he ran 15:24 to become the No. 3 seed. Kurtz also had an insane finishing kick that day. If he can replicate that performance, we think he has a real shot at taking home the title.

Cambridge is also led by Leon Ibanez-Fraile, who should be challenging the Brookline duo up front. Ibanez-Fraile is currently undefeated after big wins at the Catholic Memorial Invitational and Dual County League Meet.

Division 1B

Team Seedings

  1. Concord-Carlisle
  2. North Andover
  3. Xaverian
  4. Arlington
  5. Lincoln-Sudbury
  6. Natick

Chase Pack: Belmont, Wellesley

Although we have Concord-Carlisle at No. 1, the battle is still extremely tight. We might even go as far as to say there isn’t a clear favorite. North Andover and Concord-Carlisle are shaping up to have one of the closest battles of the day.

Both teams enter as extremely strong contenders. Concord-Carlisle has a slight edge due to its performance at the Catholic Memorial Invitational, but keep in mind that North Andover has something Concord hasn’t consistently shown this season: consistency.

Since North Andover announced itself at the Clipper Relays, we haven’t seen the team put up an underwhelming performance. Since then, they’ve conquered the Amherst Invitational, Bay State Invitational, Devens Classic, and MVC Championship, taking home four first-place finishes. Concord-Carlisle, on the other hand, has shown some vulnerability this season, specifically at the Bay State Invitational and Dual County League Meet.

We can’t tell you which team will come out on top at this point, but make sure to keep your eyes on this race because it’s going to be a close one.

Xaverian enters as the clear No. 3 seed, a bit ahead of both Arlington and Lincoln-Sudbury. They’re led by three runners who have broken the 16:00 barrier and are coming off a third-place finish at the Catholic Conference Championship.

Key Individuals

  • Jack Weintraub — Xaverian
  • Declan Hava — Natick
  • Stephen Green — Algonquin
  • Braeden Quayle — Concord-Carlisle
  • Joshua Gamel — Lincoln-Sudbury
  • Will Thomas — North Andover
  • Tuck LaRosa — North Andover
  • Noah Geiger — Belmont

Jack Weintraub is the clear favorite. The Xaverian runner has been having a great season and recently finished as the Catholic Conference runner-up, losing only to the previously mentioned Colin Kurtz. He’s also one of the state’s top returners over 800 meters, so he definitely has some serious leg speed.

We definitely wouldn’t sleep on Declan Hava, though. In a very strategic race last year, Hava battled his way to 3rd overall, making him the meet’s No. 1 returner. He’s also stepped up significantly for his team this year, going from their No. 4 runner to their No. 1. Hava is definitely a top contender and someone to watch out for.

Also be on the lookout for Concord-Carlisle’s Braeden Quayle. He actually beat Hava earlier in the season at the Catholic Memorial Invitational, so we expect him to be right up there as well.

Division 1C

Team Seedings

  1. Reading Memorial
  2. Oliver Ames
  3. Billerica Memorial
  4. Catholic Memorial

Chase Pack: Mansfield, Sharon, Plymouth South, Central Catholic, Milton

Reading is the clear favorite on paper and definitely not a team to be underestimated. After his performance at the league meet, there’s a chance we could see Declan Grant running alongside Finn Johnson-Houlihan, as he’s been their breakout runner of the season. If Reading wants to win, Max King also needs to be on the podium. King is the No. 7 returner from last year’s meet but has been having a very inconsistent season.

The Rockets also had a great showing at the Frank Mooney Invitational this past weekend, highlighted by Colin Herhily and Colin Carroll running some impressive times in less-than-favorable conditions.

Oliver Ames did get the better of Reading last year, though. In 2025, Reading entered as the No. 1 seed but lost by just four points to Oliver Ames. As the season has progressed, the Tigers have looked closer and closer to being able to accomplish the same feat. With Devon McStowe and Corey Yifru leading the way, Oliver Ames definitely has the ability to pull off another upset.

Billerica looks to take third here, with two of the fastest runners in Division 1 leading the squad. Mason Niles and Sahil Gandhi have the potential to finish 1-2 in the race, with some solid depth behind them. However, we are a bit concerned about Dylan Kane. He’s usually their No. 3 runner but didn’t compete at their conference meet. If he’s out, it puts Billerica at a disadvantage.

Catholic Memorial slides into fourth after a great showing at its conference meet. It’s unfortunate that the team finally started putting everything together just as it lost one of its top runners. Still, Catholic Memorial looks like a very strong team in the division, led by Gerard Stock, Theo Fitzpatrick, and Jude Kaleba.

Key Individuals

  • Sahil Gandhi — Billerica
  • Mason Niles — Billerica
  • Anthony Malakidis — North Attleborough
  • Devon McStowe — Oliver Ames
  • Corey Yifru — Oliver Ames
  • Finn Johnson-Houlihan — Reading
  • Declan Grant — Reading
  • Max King — Reading
  • Gerard Stock — Catholic Memorial

We’d say at least one member of the Billerica duo should be taking home the title, if not a 1-2 finish from both of them. However, their biggest competition is definitely Anthony Malakidis.

Malakidis finished third behind both runners at the Bay State Invitational and recently took home the Hockomock individual title with a PR of 15:17, running the entire race completely solo.

If Malakidis has someone to run with, we think it’s entirely possible for him to take the win.
