About MDP

Massachusetts Running, Covered Differently.

Massachusetts Distance Project is an independent running media project focused on high school cross country and distance running throughout Massachusetts.

MDP covers teams, athletes, races and stories through articles, rankings, photography and interviews — all with the goal of giving Massachusetts runners more coverage and more recognition.

Independent. Local. Free.

Built for the Massachusetts running community and focused on the athletes, teams and stories that make the sport special.
