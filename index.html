Runner of the Week

Sahil Gandhi

Billerica Memorial

After a disappointing third-place finish in a dual meet against Andover, Sahil Gandhi bounced back in a big way at Wrentham on Saturday night. He won the Bay State A race in a new Massachusetts #1 5K time of 15:14, beating a stacked field that included Mason Niles, Anthony Malakidis, and Trevor Granby.

Team of the Week

North Andover

Boys Cross Country

North Andover shocked the state this weekend, winning the Bay State B race with a statement victory over St. John’s Prep. Three runners broke 16:00, with another three under 16:30. After feeling overlooked entering the season, North Andover proved it belongs among the state’s elite, jumping from #10 to #3 in the MDP Power Rankings.

About MDP

Massachusetts Running, Covered Differently.

Massachusetts Distance Project is an independent running media project focused on high school cross country and distance running throughout Massachusetts.

MDP covers teams, athletes, races and stories through articles, rankings, photography and interviews — all with the goal of giving Massachusetts runners more coverage and more recognition.

Independent. Local. Free.

Built for the Massachusetts running community and focused on the athletes, teams and stories that make the sport special.
